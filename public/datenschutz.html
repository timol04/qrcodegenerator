In short: QR Pro runs in your browser. Your input (links, texts, logos, CSV files) is not sent to a server and not stored.

The site is delivered via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The font and libraries are loaded from this site itself, not from third parties.
